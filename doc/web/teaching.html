Thesis Projects

The projects below are written as Master of Science thesis projects and reflect my current research interests. They are aimed at students in both the Computational Science and the Quantum Science and Technology master's programmes at the University of Oslo. All of them can easily be extended to PhD projects. Each project description is available as a PDF, together with its LaTeX source. All files are collected in the ThesisProjects repository.

Courses

Morten has created and developed all the courses listed below. The courses and their material are also listed at his GitHub address.

Current courses

FYS-STK3155/4155 – Applied Data Analysis and Machine Learning

Central algorithms for statistical data analysis and machine learning, with a strong emphasis on project work. Taught by Morten since 2018.

Course page  ·  Material on GitHub

FYS4480/9480 – Quantum Mechanics for Many-Particle Systems

Many-body physics: the quantum mechanics of many-particle systems and the computational methods used to study them. Taught by Morten since the inception of the course in 2009 (except for 2014–2016).

Course page  ·  Material on GitHub

FYS4425/9425 – Computational Physics II: Reinforcement Learning and AI Agents for the Physical Sciences

The new variant of Computational Physics II, taught by Morten from the spring semester of 2027: reinforcement learning and agentic AI for the physical sciences.

Course page

FYS5419/9419 – Quantum Computing & QML

Graduate course: VQE, QPE, HHL, quantum machine learning.

Material on GitHub

FYS5429/9429 – Advanced Machine Learning

Discriminative and generative deep learning methods for the physical sciences.

Material on GitHub

Earlier courses

FYS3150/4150 – Computational Physics

Introductory computational physics course at the University of Oslo. Morten created the course and taught it from 1999 to 2022.

Material on GitHub

FYS4411/9411 – Computational Physics II

Advanced computational physics: variational Monte Carlo, machine learning for physics. Morten created the course in 2004 and taught it until the spring semester of 2026. The course is now discontinued and replaced by FYS4425/9425.

Material on GitHub

Computational Science Master's Programme

Morten initiated the Master of Science programme in Computational Science at the University of Oslo and led it from its inception in 2015 until 2025. It is a multi-disciplinary programme spanning seven departments at the Faculty of Mathematics and Natural Sciences.
