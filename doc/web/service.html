Leadership and Service at the University of Oslo

  • Since 1999: established the computational physics activity at the University of Oslo and developed its courses from scratch
  • Project leader for High-performance computing courses at UiO, 2000–2003
  • Member of the Board of USIT (Center for information technology at the University of Oslo), 2002–2004
  • Board member of the Bachelor program Mathematics, Information theory and Technology at the University of Oslo, 2002–2008
  • Leader of the Bachelor program Physics, Astronomy and Meteorology at the University of Oslo, 2002–2011
  • Together with colleagues from the Department of Physics, the Department of Mathematics and the Department of Informatics at the University of Oslo, started the Computing in Science Education project in 2004. This project, conceived back in 2003, has totally changed the way science is taught
  • Leader of the Nuclear Physics group at the University of Oslo, January 2009–December 2011
  • Initiated the Master of Science program in Computational Science at the University of Oslo and led it from its inception in 2015 until 2025. This is a multi-disciplinary program spanning seven departments at the Faculty of Mathematics and Natural Sciences
